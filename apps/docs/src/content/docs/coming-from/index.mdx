## Before you switch

- **Only one scheduler per job.** `runwisp takeover` retires cron for you. The
  `runwisp import` commands only copy your config: disable the old job yourself
  before starting it under RunWisp, or it runs twice.
- **Keep your own config apart.** `runwisp import --write` puts imported jobs in
  a separate file, and `runwisp promote` moves them into your `runwisp.toml`
  when you're ready. See
  [Staging and promoting](/coming-from/cron/#staging-and-promoting).
- **Reload after edits.** RunWisp doesn't watch files. Run
  [`runwisp reload`](/operations/reload/) after changing `runwisp.toml`.
